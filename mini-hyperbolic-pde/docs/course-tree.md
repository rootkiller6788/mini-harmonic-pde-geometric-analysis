# Course Tree — Mini Hyperbolic PDE

## Prerequisite Knowledge

```
Real Analysis → Multivariable Calculus → ODE Theory → ...
                                                    ↓
                                   Fourier Analysis → PDE Classification
                                                    ↓
                                          Linear Algebra → Hyperbolic Systems
                                                    ↓
                                  Functional Analysis → Semigroup Theory
```

## Internal Module Dependency Tree

```
Core Basic (L1+L2)
├── Classification of PDEs
├── Wave operators & characteristics
├── Domain concepts (dependence, influence, light cone)
├── Cauchy data & well-posedness
├── Energy & finite speed concepts
└── Huygens principle types

Core Objects (L3)
├── Grid structures & discretization
├── CFL condition & stability
├── Finite difference operators
├── Energy norms & invariants
└── Conservation law structures

Theorems (L4)
├── d'Alembert, energy conservation
├── Finite speed, Huygens
├── Kirchhoff, Poisson formulas
├── Existence, uniqueness, continuous dependence
└── CFL, Lax equivalence, Strichartz

Methods (L5)
├── Characteristics → Riemann problem solution
├── Energy method → uniqueness, stability
├── Fourier method → dispersion, Strichartz
└── Variational/Semigroup → abstract framework

Examples (L6)
├── 1D wave equation (various IC)
├── Transport & Burgers equations
├── Standing/traveling waves
├── Riemann problems & shocks
└── Maxwell plane waves

Applications (L7)
├── Acoustics (sound, Doppler, room modes)
├── Electromagnetism (antennas, waveguides, fiber)
├── Seismology (P/S waves, reflection)
└── Ocean/Traffic/Biomedical waves

Advanced (L8)
├── Nonlinear wave equations (null condition)
├── Shock theory (entropy, compensated compactness)
├── Einstein equations (ADM, stability)
└── Numerical methods (ENO, WENO, DG, SSP-RK)

Frontiers (L9)
├── Cosmic censorship & GR
├── Onsager & turbulence
├── Landau damping & plasma
└── ML/AI methods for PDEs
```
