# Course Alignment — 9 Universities

## MIT
- 18.152 Introduction to PDE: Laplace/Poisson equations, harmonic functions, maximum principles, Green's functions
- 18.965 Geometry of PDE: Elliptic regularity, De Giorgi-Nash-Moser, harmonic maps
- 18.155 Spectral Theory: Dirichlet/Neumann Laplacians, Weyl's law

## Stanford
- MATH 220/258 Partial Differential Equations: Harnack inequality, Liouville theorem, Schauder estimates
- MATH 286 Harmonic Analysis and PDE: Calderón-Zygmund, Hardy spaces BMO, singular integrals

## Princeton
- MAT 520 Partial Differential Equations: Perron's method, Dirichlet problem, potential theory
- MAT 560 Riemann Surfaces: Harmonic functions ≅ real parts of meromorphic functions on Riemann surfaces

## Berkeley
- MATH 222A Partial Differential Equations: Weak solutions, Sobolev spaces, Lax-Milgram
- MATH 277 Spectral Geometry: Eigenvalue problems, heat kernel, isospectrality

## Cambridge (Part III)
- Elliptic PDE: Maximum principles, Harnack, De Giorgi regularity, Schauder theory
- Geometric Analysis: Harmonic maps, minimal surfaces, Bochner formula
- Spectral Theory on Manifolds: Laplacian spectrum, Cheeger inequality

## Oxford
- B4.2 Functional Analysis and PDE: Unbounded operators, Dirichlet Laplacian
- C4.2 Geometric Analysis: Harmonic maps, Eells-Sampson, Yamabe problem
- C2.1 Lie Algebras: Spherical harmonics and representation theory

## ETH
- 401-3462 Elliptic PDE: De Giorgi-Nash-Moser theory, Krylov-Safonov estimates
- 401-4352 Spectral Theory: Weyl's law, spectral zeta function, Laplacian determinant

## ENS
- EDP Elliptiques (Elliptic PDE): Schauder estimates, maximum principles, capacity
- Analyse Géométrique (Geometric Analysis): Harmonic maps, minimal surfaces, Plateau
- Théorie du Potentiel: Martin boundary, balayage, equilibrium measures

## Tsinghua (清华)
- PDE 专题 (Special Topics in PDE): Maximum principles, Harnack, Perron's method
- 几何分析 (Geometric Analysis): Harmonic maps, Bochner formula, eigenvalue estimates
- 随机分析与PDE: Feynman-Kac, Brownian motion, Dirichlet forms
